Who We Are

KEEPING THE
TRADITIONS ALIVE

Three Aces Italian Canadian Cultural Club was founded in Toronto in 2013 by a group of friends who saw the traditions they grew up with starting to slip away — the Sunday lunches, the picnics, the whole family in one place. We started the club to keep them alive, and to keep families and their children together the way our parents and grandparents kept us together.

You don’t have to be Italian. Everybody is welcome at our table. We are volunteer-run — nobody draws a salary, and every dollar from the gear you buy here goes back into the picnic, the food drive and the community.

Annual Picnic

THE 10TH ANNUAL
THREE ACES PICNIC

One Sunday every summer at Heart Lake Conservation Area in Brampton. Pasta, pizza, bocce, a raffle, a live performer, and the pool and splash pad for the kids. Everything raised through clothing and the raffle goes straight back into the picnic and the food drive — 100%. Nine down; the tenth is going to be the big one.

Christmas Food Drive

6TH ANNUAL
CHRISTMAS FOOD DRIVE

Every December, Three Aces goes to the St. Philip Neri food bank in Toronto and hands out grocery gift cards to families who need them. 100% of what comes in goes back into the community — the gift cards and the cost of the day itself. Nobody at Three Aces takes a dollar.
